not execution authority. Do not create a new continuation branch unless main is technically
blocked by repository protection; if a temporary integration branch is unavoidable, delete

## Observation discipline

Controlled FIT inputs must contain the complete admitted subject in all eight views with
safety margin. Border contact fails the observation contract.

Evidence semantics are fixed:
`foreground -> positive`, `background -> negative`, `out-of-frame -> unknown`.
UNKNOWN may never delete geometry.

## Appearance discipline

source-provenance 2D. No unqualified lighting, material relighting, rim, bloom, tonemap or
cross-view color blending may enter the product path.

## Scientific claim discipline

generalization. New contradictions reopen only the affected scope; preserve historical
evidence append-only.
