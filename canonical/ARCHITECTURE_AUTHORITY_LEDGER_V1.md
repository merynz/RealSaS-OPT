This ledger does **not** independently authorize promotion. It answers one narrow question: **for each important mechanism, where is it implemented, what has actually been tested, and is it current product architecture or only research lineage?**

A mechanism is not considered remembered merely because a report or source file exists. It is considered reconstructed only when this ledger identifies its implementation/evidence/canonical status.

## Status vocabulary

- `BINDING` — current architectural responsibility/invariant on `main`.
- `MAINLINE_ON_HOLD` — current source exists but architecture refreeze/promotion is blocked by `CURRENT_STATE.md`.
- `IMPLEMENTED_RESEARCH` — executable challenger/mechanism exists but is not product authority.
- `TESTED_COMPONENT` — the named mechanism has controlled or diagnostic evidence, but this does not imply full-formulation validation.
- `CLOSED_REJECTED_IMPLEMENTATION` — a specific implementation failed its preregistered gate and is not promotable; the broader mechanism family may remain open unless separately falsified.
- `OPEN_REQUIREMENT` — required responsibility or diagnosis with no closed promoted solution yet.
- `FORBIDDEN` — prohibited product behavior.
