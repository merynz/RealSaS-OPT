## Cross-stage conclusions

### Mechanical/scientific compiler core — GO
Stages 01-23 are architecturally coherent enough to execute Knight without repeating the Mage authority failure. A later correction to stages 24+ does not require refitting IRIS/Geppetto/Arachne unless the S/G/W contracts themselves change.

### Mesh authority — GO
Promoting M to a first-class high-level authority remains the correct decision. M is independently qualified, B is derived exactly from W through support provenance, and runtime consumes the exact qualified M rather than minting another mesh truth.

### Strict automatic-Spine claim — NO-GO today
Material gaps:

1. **Presentation segmentation must be separate from structural partition.** One mechanical region may need multiple independently keyable slots without categorical recognition.
2. **External component masks must leave the automatic path.** Derive component observation authority from admitted observations + qualified mechanics, or explicitly change the advertised input contract.
3. **Deformation envelope must gain a generic frozen derivation/proposal+qualification path** rather than per-character canonical-joint authoring.
4. **Reusable artist preset retargeting needs generic correspondence** instead of a manual map.
5. **Editable authoring surface is not closed.** Current S/G/W/M/B/presentation/motion IRs are editable data, but Runtime-v4/.rss is an optimized playback projection with sampled posed XYZ frames; no canonical mutation/reseal/editor/export surface is part of Stage 40.

## GO / NO-GO

- **Knight stages 01-23 (render -> IRIS -> S -> Geppetto -> G -> Arachne -> W): GO**, after Stage13/14 policy values are frozen before Knight result inspection.
- **Full 01-40 as a strict “8 views -> editable automatic Spine” product claim: NO-GO today.**
- **Full 01-40 as the current controlled native animated-demo pipeline:** technically close, but it would still consume manual/hidden product inputs at Stages 25/27/34 and conflate mechanical/presentation segmentation at Stage 30.

Do not repair these gaps with Knight-specific branches or outcome-tuned thresholds. Knight remains data only.
