# Model lineage preservation — 2026-09-18

IRIS, Geppetto and Arachne are preserved on main as model families and as historical
scientific evidence. Reopening the Mage full-subject observation lineage does not invalidate
their architecture or delete their prior scoped FIT results.

Rules:

- Historical Mage checkpoints/reports stay immutable in their existing external artifact
  stores and historical branches.
- They are not silently rebound to Subject-2.
- Subject-2 receives fresh observation qualification and fresh FIT closure.
- Generic compiler/runtime contracts proven independently of Mage may be promoted to main.
- Mage-only patches, temporary product meshes, historical continuation workflows and
  subject-specific thresholds are not promoted merely to preserve history.
- A model artifact may be reused only when its input/output contract and lineage hashes make
  that reuse explicit and scientifically valid.

This preserves capability without allowing old subject authority to contaminate the new
mainline.
