## Current product authority

RealSaS V2 has three co-equal product-quality axes — **Geometry, Mechanics and Appearance** — plus an explicit role-free **Presentation** addressing authority.

- Geometry owns canonical renderable surface, topology and stable addressing.
- Mechanics owns skeleton, skin, deformation, contacts and motion.
- Appearance owns total source-preserving 2D art, provenance, static quality and intrinsic dynamic deformation conditioning.
- Presentation owns independently addressable slots/attachments/grouping from observable evidence; categorical object identity is not required or minted.

Visibility is posed canonical XYZ + camera depth. Appearance does not choose the front surface.
