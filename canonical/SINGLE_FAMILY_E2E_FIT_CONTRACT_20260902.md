# RealSaS — Single-Family End-to-End FIT Contract V1 — 2026-09-02

**Status:** `FROZEN_AFTER_ARCHITECTURE_V4__GENERALIZATION_BLOCKED_UNTIL_E2E_PASS`

## Scientific question

Can the final RealSaS architecture overfit one real clean 1024 eight-view character family strongly enough that shipping observations alone compile into a coherent editable directional 2D/2.5D puppet with a useful skeleton, qualified skinning, stable deformation and at least one effective preset animation?

This is an end-to-end existence/closure test. It is **not** a generalization test and **not** a full-3D reconstruction test.

## 3D-equivalent boundary

RealSaS may infer multi-view/world-space mechanical evidence because downstream rigging systems need mechanics analogous to what 3D systems derive from surface geometry. The target is functional equivalence:

```text
f_rig(S_hat) ~= f_rig(S_3D)
```

not reconstruction identity `S_hat == S_3D`.

The shipping product remains an eight-direction 2D/2.5D puppet. No watertight/full hidden mesh, volumetric character or 3D rigid-runtime rig is required or authorized as a rescue.

## Input boundary

Shipping inference input:

- exactly eight ordered 1024 RGBA neutral views;
- exact known camera authorities;
- no source mesh;
- no source skeleton IDs;
- no teacher weights;
- no hidden/full geometry;
- no teacher identity.

Training/evaluator lanes may consume privileged family truth under explicit leakage firewalls.

## Ordered execution

1. Freeze one immutable clean family manifest from the final FIT8 candidate pool.
2. Verify exact 8x1024 image/camera authority and character-only cleanliness.
3. Close/port the exact robust local geometry operator used by Geppetto/Arachne conditioning.
4. Run one-family IRIS fit until admitted `S_hat` is inside the known downstream mechanical-geometry tolerance or the rung fails.
5. Freeze Geppetto adapter/candidate and overfit the same family.
6. Route every proposal through Compiler qualification into `QualifiedSkeletonIR.v2`.
7. PASS mechanical-structure checks; teacher graph equality is diagnostic, not the product objective.
8. Freeze and PASS one-family SkinFieldCodec field-reconstruction/deformation ceiling.
9. Fit Arachne only after codec PASS; route output through `Compiler.qualify_skin`.
10. Build MWB-2 direction-local editable meshes from admitted support; no hidden/full-3D geometry authority.
11. Bind qualified skin to each direction-local mesh.
12. Compile observation-derived appearance for all eight directions.
13. Qualify only genuinely required unobserved visual completion; otherwise remain observation-grounded.
14. Assemble `CanonicalPuppetGraph.v3`.
15. Bind at least one deterministic puppet-local 2D/2.5D preset animation.
16. Execute exact-state proof bundle.
17. Export/runtime-project only if all required domains PASS.
18. Capture final eight-direction rest renders plus animated deformation evidence.

## PASS meaning

PASS requires one real family for which the shipping route produces a puppet that is:

- structurally coherent;
- correctly jointed enough for intended preset motion;
- skin-weighted without catastrophic collapse or implausible support;
- editable through the canonical directional mesh representation;
- stable under the required deformation probes;
- renderable in all eight required directions;
- visibly attached to the source appearance;
- capable of at least one effective preset animation;
- runtime-projectable from an exact PASS proof bundle.

PASS does **not** mean the system reconstructed the unique true 3D character.

## Forbidden rescue

Do not rescue a failure by:

- injecting teacher skeleton/weights at shipping inference;
- using hidden source mesh/full occupancy as product input;
- converting the task into full 3D reconstruction;
- bypassing Compiler qualification;
- silently generating semantic skin with BBW;
- changing family after seeing failure without recording the failure;
- claiming generalization from the one-family result.
