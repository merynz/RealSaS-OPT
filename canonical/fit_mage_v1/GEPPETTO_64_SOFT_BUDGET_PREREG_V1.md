# Geppetto 64-control soft decode budget — prereg V1

Status: `FROZEN_FOR_MEASUREMENT__NOT_A_PRODUCT_CAP`

## Policy under test

`64` is a preferred/default decode budget, not a semantic maximum and not a training-data exclusion rule.

The Geppetto architecture remains variable-cardinality. If residual mechanical evidence remains unexplained at control 64, decoding may continue up to the resource guard. No family is truncated or rejected solely for exceeding 64.

## Why this is a measurement, not a redesign

Historical FIT projection authority audited 2914 Geppetto-capable FIT assets and observed a raw deform-control maximum of 328. A former structural C0 policy at 160 admitted 2897 and put 17 assets in overflow, so a fixed product K was already known to be unsafe as an architecture invariant.

The first Mage fit also showed why raw source-bone/deform counts overstate product mechanical complexity: 41 source bones reduce to a 22-control current mechanical core after applying the generic skin-supported + required-bridge rule.

## Frozen census question

On the complete FIT/Geppetto authority population, compare:
1. raw deform-control count;
2. current mechanical-core count = controls carrying deform skin mass plus graph bridges required to connect supported controls, excluding assembly-only roots;
3. counts above 64 and above 160.

Report min/p50/p95/p99/max and `count(core>64)`. Do not modify membership, thresholds, architecture, or targets after seeing the census.

Decision rule:
- `64` may be promoted only as a default compute budget if overflow is rare and overflow decoding remains lossless/dynamic.
- `64` must never become a hard product joint cap unless a separately authorized product-domain policy explicitly excludes the overflow population.
