## 0. Decision

The demo architecture is the current RealSaS V3 product architecture. It is **not** an M4 revival and it is **not** a reduced demo architecture.

M4 is inherited selectively as:

- empirical fit/capacity evidence;
- causal evidence about useful visual/mechanical signals;
- negative evidence about failed representations/consumers;
- target/evaluator lessons;
- reusable generic neural operators when their semantics still match V3.

M4's old owner-quotient/world ontology, exact-teacher-rig product target, two-pose-specific machinery and historical product ownership are not imported merely because they once fit.

The only intentional demo-vs-generic difference remains:

```text
training distribution = one selected full-truth specimen
```

## 3. What M4 actually proved that matters now

Historical audit shows repeated strong fit/capacity results while held-out transfer remained the blocker:

- R3G: pair macro recall ~0.988-0.995; owner F1 ~0.999; component F1 ~0.998; count/multiplicity exact in tiny shared training;
- R4.99: one common model on eight heterogeneous families reached 8/8 exact, 141/141 multi-owner recovery, unsafe=0, count MAE=0, owner-parent accuracy=1.0;
- corrected full-96 eight-view shared spatial producer reached 8/8 positive safe-margin;
- the same or related formulations repeatedly failed family-disjoint image->mechanical evidence transfer despite train/tiny fit.

Therefore the demo does **not** need a weaker architecture to make fitting feasible. The fitting regime is historically supported. The hard generic research problem was invariance/transfer.

## 4. M4 lessons inherited into IRIS

Keep:

- no truth in student forward;
- exact camera geometry rather than free 3D regression;
- high-resolution local raster evidence matters;
- multiview/candidate context can be necessary;
- geometry is a strong transferable channel;
- uncertainty/support must remain explicit;
- aggregate/ranking metrics are not enough for product closure.

Do not inherit:

- owner identity as IRIS product output;
- exact teacher skeleton/parent IDs as IRIS authority;
- old GFDR scalar/object ontology;
- two-pose requirement for current neutral-8-view product input;
- candidate owner quotient as canonical geometry.

Current IRIS output remains observation-grounded forward depth/support/uncertainty; deterministic assembler owns P and S.

## 5. M4 lessons inherited into Geppetto

Geppetto receives the part of the old M4 research question that asked for a mechanical/skeleton interpretation after useful geometry/evidence exists.

Keep:

- anonymous proposal referents;
- strong spatial/multiview geometry context;
- variable cardinality / no raw teacher-K product authority;
- root and directed parent evidence;
- topology/relations as contextual mechanical state rather than only a passive auxiliary metric;
- uncertainty and safe unsupported behavior;
- global deterministic Compiler qualification;
- product skeleton need only be clean/mechanically sufficient, not source-rig exact.

Do not inherit:

- Graph-V1 free latent slot/refinement dynamics that collapsed referent diversity;
- scalar SAME threshold/components;
- irreversible learned commit authority;
- source-owner IDs or teacher helper structure;
- exact authored rig topology as product PASS.

## 6. M5 lessons inherited into Arachne

The clean historical boundary already states:


Current equivalent is `S + QualifiedSkeletonIR G -> Arachne -> SkinProposalIR -> Compiler`.

Keep:

- point/control and segment geometry;
- owner/control-context rather than naive candidate->full-F mapping;
- compact per-control field representation / SkinFieldCodec;

Do not inherit:

- source tail/helper information as product input;
- naive pointwise candidate->dense-F student;
- teacher weight closeness as the sole product criterion.
