**Status:** source and supervision audit, **no final model truth/admission**.

## Policy: record everything, infer nothing

A mesh imported without controllers may be a valid IRIS Geometry subject. A mesh with
verified native RGB/alpha may be a Graphics target. A rigged asset with verified bone
hierarchy and source motion may supply AXIS kinematic supervision. But **none of these
observations by themselves prove functional IK/FK, exact skin weights, semantic regions,
editable layer decomposition or graphics source fidelity**.

We deliberately maintain five separate ontologies:

1. **Source observation:** original SHA, native scene, importer evidence and source render.
2. **Supervision authority:** independently verified target-specific labeled examples.
3. **Capability prior:** which operations product promises to support, versioned policy.
4. **Representation policy:** which editable puppet to compile, under measurable consequences.
5. **Student inputs:** fields actually available at inference; no teacher-only source names.

## Distinct subjects vs target coverage

Target: **at least 1,000 independent FIT-subjects per model/head**, under the corresponding
head's *core* supervision contract. Advanced targets (such as native IK/FK control program,
motion-conditioned Graphics RGB, explicit layer reveal or precise MIRA intervention
consequence) have their own separately reported counts and no invented admission.
Every recorded FAIL/UNKNOWN is retained; no lowering acceptance thresholds to reach 1,000.

One source character with 76 clips and five shaders is **one subject**. LOD variants,
mirror views, different skeleton exports and augmented stylizations cannot inflate the
independent canonical source-identity denominator. Split safety applies at lineage level,
including 169 protected Master external holdouts.

## Source types and their exact supervision meaning

| Source type | Potential labels, after independent courts | Must remain UNKNOWN until tested |
|---|---|---|
| Source mesh and native materials | Geometry, appearance | Semantic class, IK and visibility |
| Mesh + verified source-face semantics | IRIS Semantic regions and instances | Animator control, required drawable policy |
| Skeleton with exact bind + skin | AXIS hierarchy, MIRA skin | Functioning IK/FK |
| Animated deform rig | AXIS pose-kinematics, deformation variants | Native authored controller graph |
| Native .blend control rig | Controller/constraint/driver architecture | Real operator response without intervention |
| Verified native controller response | Control→bone→vertex→attachment consequences | Multi-view RGB, semantic roles without separate review |
| Source-correct rendered character | IRIS Graphics original appearance | Stylized lookdev equals source truth |

**Do not use generated/upscaled/inpainted derivatives as authoritative source truth.**
Source-correct native RGB and stylized augmentation must have separate hashes,
camera/pose/renderer contracts and model target declarations. Native cameras remain
anatomically UNKNOWN until a morphology-aware orientation court.

## Native full-control pilot

Prior original imports from 2026-11-07:
- Objaverse FBX 34 bones, one action, 349 F-curves; zero imported IK constraints,
  not an authored-source IK-absence finding.
- KayKit GLB 41 bones, 76 actions, 29,252 F-curves; zero imported IK constraints,
  not an authored-source IK-absence finding.
- Quaternius OBJ no imported armature/actions; absent material dependency.

The new original `.blend` pilot targets Mesh2Motion's source-art repo pinned at
`dc2eb3cf06ba0aaeb0c651503d5667f773781522`; native snake, spider and dragon
rigs are inspected independently with Blender 4.5.14, source SHA256 and
`--disable-autoexec`. This is a distinct **nonhuman control-source gold pilot**, not
3,000 training examples or 3 full native-IK PASS. The upstream repo says models,
rigs and animations were manually created and CC0; full source/content and
dependency validation must still be preserved.

Blender Studio Rain v3, Snow v4, Critter v1 and One v1 are other carefully selected
authored `.blend` controller candidates. Official documentation identifies IK/FK
switching, snapping, reverse FK, pose libraries and/or custom correctives, depending
on the character. **They are not full-control certified by this project's tests.**
Some require Blender 5.x or Blender Studio account access. Each CC-BY grant requires
proper attribution/source receipts and separate licensing review.

Tripo3D delivers skeleton and skin as rigged exports, not demonstrated authored
full IK/FK control graphs. Kavya explicitly advertises real skeletal 2D bones,
IK, physics and path constraints. **These are product benchmarks, not sources of
training assets**, unless independent authorized sources are obtained.

## Required future milestones — order cannot be inverted

**A. Recover and rights-classify:** 32-shard run 42374046073 recovered or reverified
3,192 of 3,214 eligible originals (19 404, 3 64-MiB-cap exceptions); 135
historical missing holdout identities were purposely excluded. Reconcile
canonical source duplicates, rights, texture and dependency completeness.

**B. Classify real characters and source structure:** whole scene, object vs
connected island vs anatomical component, source rest, pose, orientation,
cameras, skin/rig/controls, attachments, native material and alpha.

**C. Independent observation courts:** scene and camera parity, 8-view
contact sheets, source-normal/depth/primitive face maps, rig interventions
(positive and negative control effects), carefully reviewed semantics,
visual human QA with underlying source face receipts.

**D. Target-specific truth:** Geometry / Semantic / Graphics independent
masks and losses; TESSA animation-ready surface topology and deformation;
AXIS kinematics, controls and consequences where available; MIRA exact
influence weights and deformations. Every source-target pair has its own
PASS, FAIL, UNKNOWN, MASKED or NOT_APPLICABLE and evidence SHA.

**E. Separate admission, materialization and training:** no record becomes
admitted merely because source import, CI or external dataset registration
succeeded. Source → court → target → consumer/loss binding → independent
admission → materialization → training.

Nothing in this branch modifies the production compiler, IRIS training,
active Corpus370 pipeline or existing Master source recovery PR.
