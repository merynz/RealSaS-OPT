# Expanded source inspection

This is a source census and selective reuse continuation of PR311. It does not
start a training run, create a split, register an Attempt, or grant admission.
The historical 139 rows retain their original source hashes and IDs. Publisher
export formats and Sushi equipped variants do not become additional subjects.

The measured candidate denominator is 370 rows: 139 established rows and 231
expansion candidates. All 370 were imported through the animation-free controlled
Blender importer with the pinned official Blender 4.0.2 executable. Each row has
raw-source, producer and output hashes, imported rest geometry, mesh ownership,
authored control matrices and sparse skin weights. Runtime libc is recorded;
these imports do not establish parity with historical admitted imports.

The last row is a topological prerequisite, not a global SDF or animation-ready
mesh count. Self-intersection, nesting, the chosen assembly boundary, raw/import
equivalence, motion responses and consumer courts remain separate. Mesh ownership
is an authored address, not a semantic label or evidence of modularity.

The independent MeshLab intersection probe inspected all 69 closed-component
candidates: 49 scenes had intersecting faces; 7 contained a component with detected
self-intersection. These counts overlap. The probe is a diagnostic, not a solid
assembly/nesting qualification, and does not authorize any global sign rows.

Coincident-position inspection found duplicate positions within a single owner in
279 candidates. Five candidates contain at least one such group with conflicting
normalized skin weights at the declared 1e-6 diagnostic threshold. Rest position
coincidence must not silently authorize a mechanical weld. Equal weights alone
also do not replace full source/import/deformation equivalence.

Historical evidence was also reopened without regeneration: all 973 ledger refs
matched their hashes; all 139 geometry and RGB courts passed. The additional check
reconstructed all 32,768 surface rows and 8,192 addressed distance rows per subject
and independently queried 96 nearest distances per subject. Oriented source
triangle-position multisets matched fresh imports for all 139; 128 also matched
vertex/face arrays in their original order. Row order is not surface identity, and
this geometric comparison does not claim full admitted import/skin equivalence.
All 139 historical geometry manifests contain zero valid global sign rows.

All 139 historical TESSA teachers selected SOURCE_FALLBACK and are rejected by the
current court with LEGACY_CANDIDATE_REEVALUATION_REQUIRED. Geometry/RGB numerical
reuse candidates and mechanical teacher qualification are separate outcomes.

## Reproduction from recovered source and inventory inputs

1. Run `compile_expanded_subject_candidates_v1.py` with the exact historical
   source manifest, recovered base source root, publisher structure/admission
   inventories, publisher workspaces and additional-source acquisition receipt.
2. Run `run_expanded_teacher_source_inspection_v1.py` with the candidate JSON,
   pinned Blender binary and output directory. A cache hit requires the source,
   all importer producer files and both output files to match. Completion checks
   every subject report; a Blender process exit alone cannot declare success.
3. Run `summarize_teacher_source_inspection_v1.py` with that candidate JSON and
   inspection directory. Independent structural and component kernels measure
   each imported mesh. Per-row routes preserve unknown/missing truth explicitly.

Inputs and receipts are explicit CLI arguments. The tools never assume 139,
370, a train/unseen allocation or a qualified family count. The 370-row checkpoint
contains all imported NPZ payloads, all inspection/structure reports, the candidate
roster and recovery receipts. Raw assets remain unmodified.

## Sign and the StdGEN++ comparison

StdGEN++ section IV-D supervises reconstruction using layered image, mask,
semantics, depth and normal renders; its mesh stage extracts semantic-equivalent
SDF with FlexiCubes and adds a sign-related hole regularizer. Its published
training description does not justify relabeling nearest-component sign as a
global solid label. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07660v1 (IV-C/IV-D).

For this corpus, retain exact-source surface/UDF and renderer evidence where its
own courts pass. An explicit global SDF target requires a qualified solid domain
and distances to that domain's actual boundary. Missing sign stays missing.
Do not throw away useful open/modular source evidence, silently close a raw mesh,
or declare topology/deformation valid because a teacher selected SOURCE_FALLBACK.

The two requested end-to-end truth/training notebooks and final training counts
remain unfinished. This finite census supplies their subject inspection input;
notebook execution cannot replace Platform admission or a typed consumer court.
