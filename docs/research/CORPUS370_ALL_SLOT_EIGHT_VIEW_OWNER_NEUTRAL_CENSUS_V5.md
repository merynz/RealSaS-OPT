## Why V5

Its limitation was sampling **two selected operational slots out of eight** per subject, all in rest pose at 192×192. Source-hidden graphics were not lost in the anchor-pair test, but six other slots remained unmeasured individually.

The V5 all-slot test preserves the same immutable V5 observation index, original source/roster, original rest geometry, native visibility join and all-view geometry compiler proposal. ## Admission and scientific interpretation

A successful research run must verify every source slot has a distinct, repeatable removal arm at every camera; exact source and proposal identities; eight bounded source/soup + no-op parity views; and the independently courted anchor B reveal after removing A. The number of slots with any visible direct effect is **reported separately**. An entirely occluded or offscreen slot may have zero direct pixels yet remain legitimate; that is `ABSTAIN` or `UNKNOWN`, never `DIFFERENT_DRAWABLE`.

This experiment **cannot** prove authored drawable identity, mechanical causality, full native-resolution CAA, skinned multi-pose persistence, materialization readiness or IRIS/TESSA training. It may reveal geometry-only grouping failures and false confidence from one chosen pair, but its own numeric group count is not an optimized puppet representation.
