## Why this artifact is necessary

Our compiler's output is a source-authoritative **editable puppet** with 3D geometry and mechanics and 2D presentation semantics. Mechanical response groups are not necessarily drawables; even geometrically local depth order is not always a globally renderable layer order. The IR needs to retain typed original-source observations, provenance, observability and UNKNOWN **without preemptively deciding the model head, number of parts or algorithm**. StdGEN/Bunraku are representational references; neither category-specific semantic heads nor teacher-source artist layer names are generic truth.
