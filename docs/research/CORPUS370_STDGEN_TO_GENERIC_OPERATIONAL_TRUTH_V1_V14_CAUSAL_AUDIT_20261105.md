## External method: what StdGEN actually does

The original CVPR 2025 StdGEN uses semantic-aware triplane S-LRM reconstruction with multi-layer supervised RGB/mask/depth/normal and explicit semantic-surface extraction. Its official Blender teacher physically inspects VRM object/material names such as Hair, Face, CLOTH, Body and SKIN and assigns body/clothing/hair/face labels. It renders complete and selectively peeled source, sometimes substitutes controlled palette colors, and supplies semantic masks/depth and normal. Its semantic layer count reflects its restricted annotation ontology, not a universal mathematically identifiable puppet partition. The 2026 StdGEN++ builds dual full-body/face branches, finer 3D extraction and editable facial appearance. Their real 3D mechanics and layered graphics must not be dismissed as ordinary 2D segmentation.

Sources: https://github.com/hyz317/StdGEN/blob/main/blender/blender_lrm_script.py ; https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/He_StdGEN_Semantic-Decomposed_3D_Character_Generation_from_Single_Images_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf ; https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07660 .
