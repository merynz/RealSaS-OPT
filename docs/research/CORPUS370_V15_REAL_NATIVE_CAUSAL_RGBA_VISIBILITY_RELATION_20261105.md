## Scoring

For each identical-source control, pose, view and perturbation sign, compare:
1. Whole original FULL scene baseline vs original control ±, represented by mask E.
2. The corresponding original causal-active source-face-only baseline vs same original control ±, represented by mask I.

Differences use maximum of absolute alpha and premultiplied RGB channel deltas; threshold 0.025. Pure transparent RGB differences have zero consequence.

The three disjoint cases are E∩I, I minus E, E minus I. We label I minus E only **ISOLATION_ONLY / HIDDEN_OR_COMPOSITING_CANDIDATE**, not a proven occluded surface. E minus I is **WHOLE_SCENE_CONTEXTUAL_COMPOSITING_CANDIDATE**, not new author layer. A source control causing no visible change in one sampled original scene is UNKNOWN to an observer of that scene, not a qualified absent-control negative.
