## What this is not

- A complete physically composited ray-to-image proof: a geometric front hit is NOT a visible alpha contributor without a qualified original material transparency, camera raster pixel sampling, z/alpha and shading model.
- An original author SAME_DRAWABLE/DIFFERENT_DRAWABLE oracle. Geometry face, source object, bone action and source color are not art owners.
- An annotation scheme for absent image evidence. A source ray present at alpha-zero, an occluded element or an unseen back layer remains UNKNOWN or ABSTAIN; never source-drawable negative.
- An unseen-family trial or 352-subject training admission. This is a 3-morphology exploratory diagnostic, with explicit original source lineage.
- A substitute for graphics-independent edit consequence, native CAA, original draw order, influence training or a consumer PuppetRevision.

## Exit gate for operational graphics truth

Require exact original-source full-image/soup fidelity, source-geometry ray/pose-pixel alignment, real alpha and ordered ray compositing, independent addressable source-face edits with held-out pose/view, negative controls, over-edit and under-edit cost, stable anonymous (permutation-invariant) layer grouping, source image faithful appearance and masked unknowns. Then separately prove that model-visible inputs predict those editable operational parts without privileged source bone/artist labels. Until then graphics-owner, all model-head training and materialization authority remain FALSE.
