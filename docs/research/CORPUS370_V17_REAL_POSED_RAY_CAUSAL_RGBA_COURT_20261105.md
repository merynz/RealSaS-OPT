## Why the fresh source rays matter

The old V5 source-locus all-hit geometric ray oracle used rest geometry. Since V13b/V16 demonstrated actual source deformation—including simultaneous A+B nonadditivity—those rays cannot be reused under authored motion. V17 reads the actual **V16 original A+B posed world-space vertices**, the exact original source faces and the V14 SHA-locked camera. It selects source-pose triangle centroid pixels without future color/alpha/owner labels and measures the entire geometric ray stack for **3 authored poses × 4 source views × 4 signed joint interventions** for each original character. Each case has up to 12 deterministic source-geometric ray proposals, total **576 per source**.

At the identical pixel after the Blender bottom-up image buffer conversion, it replays the actual V14 single original source-control effects and the V16 original simultaneous A+B **premultiplied RGB and alpha**. It does not infer artist slots or refer to source bone names as model features. A second independent Python court recomputes the triangle hits, barycentric→world ray-point, ordered depths, complete/tie flags, geometry-only sample list and alpha/counterfactual effect against the exact V16 source array. All raw per-pixel original art/face/barycentric content remains private on Drive.

## What needs to be falsified next

**181** source geometry-selected pixel-center queries lack an independently qualified complete untied nonempty ray stack. **132** of the actual observed changed image pixel instances live on these rays. A direct pixel-center ray missing a thin triangle while the raster anti-alias filter responds is plausible; so is a camera/raster mapping or source material-compositing mismatch. **We cannot silently count either as no surface, absent ownership, or proven occlusion.** The independent stage emits UNKNOWN and no training authority. A subsequent source subpixel geometric-coverage and alpha/blend court must disentangle raster coverage from true unobservability (potentially per-pixel 2×2 or 4×4 and exact filtered source-alpha response). Never 'repair' a geometric ray hit list by nearest-neighbor source face assignment without physical projection witness.

Even a complete geometric ray stack only establishes triangle *intersection order*. It cannot prove actual artist draw order or alpha-compositing of textures. A source artist may use UV alpha masks, transparent surface overlaps and graphic layers not correlated with a single contiguous mesh, material ID or bone. The proper next **graphics operational owner** experiment is independent candidate-layer edit/reveal/recolor at heldout authored poses/views with source appearance parity, occlusion/alpha and edit collateral, compared across variable K/morphologies. V7/V12 already establish that fixed K proxy labels do not generalize; do not repeat those experiments or use their finest partition as the gold human drawable truth.
