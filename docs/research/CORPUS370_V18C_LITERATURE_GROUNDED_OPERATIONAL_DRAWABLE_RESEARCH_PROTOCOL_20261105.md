## North-star contract

RealSaS compiles an editable puppet, not merely segmentation, pixels, a fixed semantic ontology, or animation frames. We need a source-derived, anonymous, variable-cardinality, persistent and independently editable **operational graphics decomposition** that composes into source-authoritative presentation across poses/views/motions. Mechanics, geometry, graphics and causal correspondence are **distinct** truth families. The source rig/artist naming, materials, armature and author-defined scene tree are private measurement instruments, not student-visible labels.

Teacher discovery is not the same as final target admission:
`source SHA + rest geometry + qualified observation -> physical source intervention -> independent edited RGBA / ray / occlusion evidence -> explicit operational relations + uncertainty -> source-consistent candidate decomposition -> independently heldout edit/render consequence court -> canonical model-consumer binding and lineage-safe training admission`.

The artist's original layer decomposition is not generally uniquely identifiable from image evidence. Our goal is a **minimal adequate, consumer-editable operational representation**, potentially with multiple equivalent admitted decompositions, not recovery of an unknowable original author grouping. Do not convert "different motion", "same material", "same bone", "connected face", "source object", "same color" or "invisible edit" directly into artist SAME/DIF DRAWABLE. Keep UNKNOWN/ABSTAIN typed.

### Root cause taxonomy before further experiments

**TRANSPORT/RENDER INVALID**: original source/soup no-op mismatch, texture/material/alpha/pose/view/draw-order parity failure. No segmenter score.
**PROBE UNOBSERVABLE**: counterfactual source edit is beneath raster visibility/subpixel resolution or occluded and remains unexposed. Only UNKNOWN, not similarity or good collateral score.
**PROBE OBSERVABLE, RELATION AMBIGUOUS**: visible edit support but different valid source-layer representations possible. Store consequence, avoid artist claim.
**COUNTERFACTUAL DISAGREEMENT**: observed edit yields independently measurable missing edits, nonlocal collateral, alpha seam or visibility/order regression against a *declared edit intent*; this is evidence against **that operational candidate**, not necessarily artist grouping.
**QUALIFIED**: all required base/pose/view/edit/heldout/coverage checks PASS and canonical/model consumer mapping tested; only then contemplate training masks.

## Relevant literature — what transfers and what does not

| Reference | Verified method | Potential transfer to RealSaS | Explicit limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Felzenszwalb & Huttenlocher, *Efficient Graph-Based Image Segmentation*, IJCV 2004 | Adaptive internal-vs-external dissimilarity for spatial graph components | Diagnostic unsupervised local baseline | Attractive only; chaining still possible; not editable graphics |
| Keuper et al., *Efficient Decomposition of Image and Mesh Graphs by Lifted Multicuts*, ICCV 2015 | Signed correlation clustering with nonlocal lifted edges; 3D mesh applications | Candidate long-range constraints and globally consistent variable-K partitions | General optimization NP-hard; meaningful signed costs must come from trustworthy experiments |
| Wolf et al., *The Mutex Watershed: Efficient, Parameter-Free Image Partitioning*, ECCV 2018; *The Mutex Watershed and its Objective*, TPAMI 2020 | Descending weighted attraction/repulsion decisions, dynamically inherited cannot-link, no required seed/K | Fast signed sparse-graph baseline, addresses V18a's unconstrained chaining | "Parameter-free" applies to partition inference, **not** creation/calibration of reliable attractive/repulsive cues; greedily prioritized evidence may be wrong |
| Levinkov et al., *Higher-Order Multicuts for Geometric Model Fitting and Motion Segmentation*, TPAMI 2022 | Hyperedge / any-order costs beyond pairwise, approximate local optimization | Candidate nonlinear joint-control and triple-locus consistency terms | Costly/NP-hard and easy to overfit tiny source pilot; only after clean pairwise evidence |
| Yang et al., *MovingParts: Motion-based 3D Part Discovery in Dynamic Radiance Field*, ICLR 2024 | Lagrangian trajectories and part-level rigid-motion factorization | Motion correspondence and mechanically coherent candidate primitives | Motion parts are not necessarily graphics drawables, especially deformable/skinned surfaces |
| Lin et al., *SplArt*, ICCV 2025 | Category-agnostic articulated reconstruction via differentiated 3DGS mobility and staged optimization | Anatomy-free motion-supervision ideas, primitive-level mobility | Mostly articulation and 3D reconstruction; not layer compositing/independent 2D editor truth |
| Kasten et al., *Layered Neural Atlases for Consistent Video Editing*, TOG 2021 | Per-layer alpha and persistent 2D atlas coordinates across frames; edits propagate through occlusion/deformation | Operational **edit consistency**, visibility/alpha/temporal identity as a downstream graphic criterion | Per-video learned layer atlases, not a source-certified generic 3D puppet partition |
| Monnier et al., *Unsupervised Layered Image Decomposition into Object Prototypes*, ICCV 2021 | Explicit alpha sprites, transformation and ordered compositing | Presentation reconstruction/occlusion failure cases | Object prototypes differ from deformable puppet charts and original-source artist truth |
| Damaraju et al., *CObL: Toward Zero-Shot Ordinal Layering without User Prompting*, ICCV 2025 | Variable-count, ordered, amodally complete object layers, inference-time compositing constraint | Target form suggests unknown/backside/order handling | Generative completion is inference, NOT observed exact-source truth; synthetic domain |
| He et al., *StdGEN: Semantic-Decomposed 3D Character Generation from Single Images*, CVPR 2025; StdGEN++ arXiv 2026 | Semantic-aware representation/head + multilayer extraction; enhanced editable appearances | Future learnable **head design** may be adapted to anonymous relation/affinity and support/visibility fields | Their restricted semantic ontology is not a generic author-neutral layer truth, and generated completion cannot become exact ground truth |
| Locatello et al., *Object-Centric Learning with Slot Attention*, NeurIPS 2020 | Exchangeable object-like slots competing for perceptual support | Later possible variable-cardinality, permutation-invariant student interface | Good slot learning does not itself establish editable truth or distinct graph mechanics |

**Choice:** move beyond FH-only. Favor **Mutex Watershed as first inexpensive diagnostic after signed edges are qualified**, then **Lifted Multicut** for nonlocal consistency; add Higher-Order Multicut only if real A+B interaction demonstrably identifies constraints missed by pairwise costs. Keep V18a thresholded components and FH as honest baselines. Do not proclaim either candidate the truth algorithm before edit-consequence gates.

## Representation and learnability invariants

1. Primitive identity: exact source triangle + barycentric/UV/material chart + source SHAs **in private teacher evidence only**; source triangle may be too small to serve as a drawable. Maintain cross-pose locus correspondence and duplicate/overlapping alpha contributions; do not force a single partition when the compositor needs layered overlaps.
2. Proposed drawable group: anonymous variable-K index, permutation-invariant. A single graphics group can respond to many mechanics controls; one control can deform many graphics groups. Never couple graphics K to rig bone K, mesh object K, or semantic category count.
3. Graphics authority must track **alpha, appearance, source-view visibility, ordered K-hit evidence, seams, occlusion/reveal, attachment and persistence**. Visibility/draw order may be view- and pose-dependent. NO fabricated globally fixed depth order; no unobserved layer completion treated as source fact.
4. Source evidence should produce typed `ATTRACT`, `REPEL`, `UNKNOWN` relations with source/control/view/time validity masks and confidence. DIFFERENT_MOTION is not DIFFERENT_DRAWABLE; SAME_MOTION is not SAME_DRAWABLE. Contradictory edges must fail or abstain, never silently resolve in favor of visual aesthetics.
5. Use the same source-face observations, pose0/1 fit controls and heldout pose2/camera/edit budget for all candidate solvers. No algorithm may consume V18b heldout *outcome* data to fit its graph and then report that same outcome as blind generalization.
6. Student input remains source-independent observed RGB(A), cameras and current evidence; teacher-only rig, source triangle indices and intervention outcomes enter **targets and independent courts only**. Any future semantic-inspired head predicts anonymous affinities/slots with masks, not imported source names.
7. Admit a **set of functionally equivalent decompositions** or only partial pairwise constraints where data do not identify a unique grouping. Canonical slot indexes cannot be global identity labels. A shape-conditioned numerical loss with shuffled slot ordering needs permutation-invariant binding and its own proof.

- Preserve `ARTIST_ORIGINAL_DRAWABLE_UNKNOWN` even when operational grouping qualifies; source-created edit equivalence, not artist semantics, is our truth scope.
- Bind accepted source-domain operational graphics targets and typed validity masks to a canonical scene domain and independent Graphics/Relations consumer; establish lineage-safe subject splits. Materialization is separate and requires its own receipt. No current authority flag changed.
- Only afterward compare IRIS oracle-target vs predicted-target vs full Compiler consequence and unseen generalization. A learnt semantic-style head may become anonymous slot/affinity/uncertainty head, but this is architecture research **after truth**.

## Explicit falsifiers / stop conditions

- All original source render-parity gates cannot be reproduced on exact source SHA -> stop candidate comparison and debug renderer.
- Source-target edits remain unobservable -> no operational pair labels; measure coverage failure and seek genuinely independent source-ray evidence.
- Mutex/Multicut produce fewer or prettier groups without better heldout edit outcomes -> no promotion.
- High-confidence `REPEL` comes only from distinct motion signatures without edit conflict -> invalid source of negative supervision.
- Hidden pixels are hallucinated, cached source labels leak into student inputs, thresholds tuned on heldout, or render authority forged -> quarantine evidence.
- Multiple proposal groupings are edit-equivalent -> preserve ambiguity and equivalent targets; do not invent unique artist truth.
