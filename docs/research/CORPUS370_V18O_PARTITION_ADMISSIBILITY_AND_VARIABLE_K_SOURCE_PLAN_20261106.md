The RealSaS product is the editable, identity-preserving *puppet* and 2D presentation, not a sprite generator. Geometry and mechanics are distinct from operational **drawable groups**. The Graphics compiler must derive independently controllable drawables that preserve UV/source appearance, view- and sample-local coverage, per-ray depth/occlusion, cross-pose identity and heldout removal/motion/reveal consequences. V18l/m/n conclusively address a **restricted source renderer mechanism** on Cat and Triceratops, not the more difficult **drawable partition discovery** problem.

## Next evidence gate: change candidate science before expensive source rendering

The *next experimental target* is causal operational drawable proposal discovery, not a mass renderer replay:
1. Build source-only *signed local and long-range graph evidence* separating a same-control deformation response from source-local occlusion/appearance effects. A body part may move together across controls but still be independently drawable; therefore **do not equate skin weights, source mesh connectivity, or same-bone influence with graphic ownership**.
2. Compare three frozen morphology-agnostic grouping baselines under identical source fit/heldout split: current V18a reactive-connectivity, source-signed Mutex Watershed, and nonlocal Lifted Multicut with geometry/render-consistency costs. Do not use source semantic part names, per-subject tuning or original heldout edit outcomes as fitting data.
3. **Independent heldout physical edit court** on the same original source asset and blind V11 targets: require selected groups have a nontrivial visible edit consequence without deleting most unrelated source; count overedit collateral, underedit/reveal, stability across camera and authored pose, and UNKNOWN where evidence does not support an ownership decision. Compare real original source native/soup materials and alpha, not colored masks alone.
4. Only then promote successful variable-K source group visibility to a real Blender K>=3 oracle-free compositing source court. A separate original source with actual nonbinary or stochastic transparent alpha is REQUIRED for alpha-generalization; current Cat/Triceratops are binary 1spp controls.
5. Generic IRIS/TESSA supervised consumer target authority stays blocked until independent source evidence supports compatible outputs; do not add arbitrary artist drawable IDs by hand.

This staged decision avoids both premature "we solved decomposition" and an unbounded new multihour render run on known highly degenerate source groups.
