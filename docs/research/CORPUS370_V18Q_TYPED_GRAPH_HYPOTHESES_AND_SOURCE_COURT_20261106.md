## Why this work is necessary

V18p established that Cat has 260 separate source face edge-topology islands before any reactive-stain separation (V18a .10 K288), whereas Triceratops is one source edge-topology component, and the pooled response RMS at .10 yields K22. In Triceratops 844/2001 source-adjacent face edges have an above-.10 distance on at least one axis, yet only 117 edges fail the pooled all-channel RMS. This is an ablation of measured mechanical information, not count of correct graphic boundaries.

The research decision is to **stop equating response with owner**:
- SAME_RESPONSE does not imply SAME_DRAWABLE.
- DIFFERENT_RESPONSE does not imply DIFFERENT_DRAWABLE.
- SOURCE_VERTEX_EDGE_DISCONNECTED does not imply DIFFERENT_DRAWABLE.
- SOURCE_VERTEX_EDGE_CONNECTED does not imply SAME_DRAWABLE.
- Correct per-sample source depth compositing, as seen in V18m/n, does not prove an artist edit group.

The missing source-scientific input is **source surface geometric proximity / UV-material continuity / cross-view appearance and occlusion constraints**. V18q MUST report those as UNOBSERVED, not fill them from a prior or fabricate a result. Only after that gap is filled can a signed graph optimizer be given meaningful evidence.
