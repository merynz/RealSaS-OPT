## Research goal and epistemic firewall

Discover **what the original character actually establishes** about source geometry, articulation, appearance, ray-local visibility, surface correspondence and intervened edit consequences. Do **not** decide a puppet layer count or student head by fitting a source-private 3D graph. A compiler representation is a consumer of truth, not the definition of source truth.

Vocabulary:
- `OBSERVED_SOURCE`: immutable source-local measurement, exact source SHA + pose/view/control/face sample provenance.
- `DERIVED_QUALIFIED`: consequence derived through independently qualified operation / ray / source physical sample court.
- `HYPOTHESIS_NOT_LABEL`: retrieval / affinity / potential grouping used to choose an experiment.
- `UNKNOWN`: source does not identify the relationship in this observational/intervention scope.
- `CONTRADICTED_IN_DECLARED_CLASS`: a specific tested representation / operation hypothesis has a physical counterexample. This is NOT a universal different-drawable label.

## Strongest new conceptual conclusion

The evidence joins into **three different relations on the same source loci**, none a synonym for another:

1. **Mechanical relation:** response under each independently authored control and pose. It can be smooth across a deforming mesh while visual/UV edit domains split, or differ within one continuous painted surface.
2. **Geometric/render relation:** local surface adjacency/proximity and ray depth, view/sample-specific visible contribution, alpha/compositing. A source vertex-edge island or material chart alone is not a drawable.
3. **Intervention relation:** independently prescribed source appearance edits, remove/reveal/move operations, their observed physical RGBA change/support and validity. This can test specific editable-operation claims without recovering original artist intent.

The product compiler will later select an editable representation conditioned on a **capability contract** and complexity policy. The IRIS student may eventually learn qualified observation targets with validity masks. Neither choice redefines the source truth.
