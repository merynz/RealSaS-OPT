# RSS.vNext — Model ↔ Compiler evidence flow: mechanics, ownership, graphics and 2D presentation

**Date:** 2026-11-13. **Status:** evidence-routing audit, not a finished model architecture, approval to train, or puppet product qualification. **Reviewed repo:** `merynz/RealSaS-OPT@40aa72d10a9ddc575be9e490f77ae0a65b8b3498` plus research PR #484. No assumption that an unseen-trained checkpoint exists. This study is independent of the parallel dataset campaign.

## Guiding reverse dependency

`Executable user-visible behavior → runtime fields → deterministic Compiler computation → minimal missing model evidence → model-access/learning stage → independent source/native/pose teacher + mask → proof gate`.

This order rejects two opposite mistakes:
1. expecting Compiler to discover what object is held, what bends, what covers another drawable, which rear pixels exist or what designer intended merely by optimizing geometry;
2. training a giant all-pairs semantic ontology to predict operations that can be computed once the necessary *addressed* relations and geometric evidence exist.

A single checkpoint may be learned in ordered stages. One shared feature field does not require one homogeneous spatial support, a single universal loss, or all expert heads to train simultaneously. AniGen has a surface/skeleton dual support with coupled fields, SkinTokens has a frozen FSQ codec dependency, and StdGEN has source-conditioned geometry/appearance/semantic stage progression. These are whole training programs, not drop-in heads.

## B. Topology ownership is a distinct signal, not a semantic synonym

Topology can **deterministically** prove: faces share a vertex/edge; connected surface islands; which TESSA-predicted component a face belongs to; geometric local normal/area; whether a proposed mesh violates adjacency/degeneracy. It may **constrain hypotheses** about separately editable material patches. It cannot prove: what constitutes a sword, that a sword is *held*, how that object is detached, that connected cloth is a separate layer, or whether motion must be rigid.

The current producer already emits `faces`, `vertex_components`, `face_components` in `animation_ready_mesh.py`; `rss_vnext_surface_decisions_v1.py` materializes all corner-level expert evidence; and `rss_vnext_relation_epistemic_court_v1.py::derive_candidate_topology_relations_v1` computes `SHARES_INDEXED_EDGE` and `SAME_CANDIDATE_COMPONENT` **without** a new learned head. Thus do not teach these exact candidate primitives again. **Hypothesis to test:** use structural component/edge evidence to condition *learned* drawable/attachment confidence, with source-qualified edit/transport labels. Do not threshold region slots to invent semantic cut classes.

### Specific falsification examples

1. Two contiguous facial shading regions may be different graphic material charts yet one mechanical surface, one drawable or two overlays depending on actual edit consequence. A material boundary is not a compulsory cut.
2. Wrist/arm/hand may be one connected skinned surface with large joint influence gradient; separating at a region label destroys bending even though labels differ.
3. A prop may consist of disconnected decorative submeshes but be a single attachment moving rigidly under one socket; connected-components count is not object count.
4. A hovering prop near a hand is not held; one frame or nearest-surface is insufficient. Multiple source pose relative transforms or authored parent metadata distinguish attachment from proximity.
5. An opaque back-side is hidden in original FRONT/BACK and may remain unknown even if model produces a plausible color; predicted color must never get original-pixel ownership.
6. A soft appendage labeled 'cape' that does not have temporal/source response cannot learn physically true spring parameters from static labels; choose policy defaults as policy, not truth.

## C. Readiness and training dependency graph

1. **Source-3D shared geometry/appearance/semantic foundation** (StdGEN-style pretraining). Verify exact checkpoint architecture, memory/precision, field/mesh gradients and qualified source-layer targets. No arbitrary `strict=False` transfer.
2. **Surface topology/ownership** conditioned on queryable scene H plus geometry+semantic evidence, with face grammar/topology truth and motion-aware candidate ranking **only for eligible source pose supervision**. A separate skeleton-interior support is an AniGen/Rigel3D-backed *ablation*: compare loss/gradient/signal preservation to existing triplane H under equal compute; triplane may be replaced if evidence supports it.
3. **MIRA SkinAE/FSQ codec** on exact source mesh/joint/skin conditioning. Freeze a reproducible codec for **AXIS** token labels. Evaluate active influence precision/recall, hard-code vs soft expected-code LBS behaviour and unknown masks on each morphology family.
4. **AXIS causal control/anchor/socket program** trained with qualified source rig, coordinate frames, DOF, constraint and relative transport labels. No teacher joint/pose enters student input. Separate forbidden/absent/unknown attachment supervision.
5. **Typed relational/mechanical and 2D appearance evidence** from shared H/TESSA/AXIS/MIRA with independently masked source editability/visibility/pose witnesses; do **not** train all relations merely because all pair labels exist. Source run may yield 3D topology but no authored 2D artistic order.
6. **Compiler qualification and runtime**: verify exact source/camera/checkpoint/mesh lineages, source-first-hit/CAA appearance, selective physical and artistic ordering, explicit surface binding and action simulation. Verify pick → move → rotate → move/reveal → save as same puppet. Product readiness is downstream of all courts.

One final checkpoint may contain the fused experts even though training is sequential and certain codebooks frozen first. **No capacity decision (e.g., enlarging MIRA FSQ, replacing IRIS with sparse voxels, or adding a specialty head) is accepted before paired ablation with quality, GPU budget, gradient conflicts and exact Compiler consequence.**

## D. Current blocking seams (concrete source audit)

- **AXIS socket attachment:** program has candidate control and anchor pointers; output preserves raw events, but no socket→held/holder face ownership or qualified contact is implied. The new readout only fixes the *evidence transfer*; the connection to `AttachmentSurfaceBindingV1` still needs explicit held/holder proposal plus independent relative-motion evidence. `derive_surface_binding(..., holder_face_indices=None)` performs an unbounded rest-nearest selection within component owners: **do not use that fallback to discover a product attachment from neural evidence**.
- **Drawables:** typed pair training exists in `training_engine.py`, with three distinct trusted source channel receipts: `QUALIFIED_SOURCE_EDIT_DRAWABLE_CONSEQUENCE`, `QUALIFIED_SOURCE_GEOMETRIC_SEPARABILITY`, `QUALIFIED_SOURCE_CONTACT_AND_MOTION_INTERVENTION`. Source co-region membership trains a DIFFERENT loss. These code paths exist, but qualified data coverage and trained predictive accuracy are **not proven**.
- **Two-view hidden appearance:** `source_ray_evidence.py` samples source RGBA but explicitly lacks source-depth authority. Source-native/qualified first-hit ray stacks and source CAA are different courts; novel-view synthesized texture stays predicted, not source.
- **Secondary:** `affordance_logits` and source temporal-response tools exist, but generic native secondary dynamics under source-known/two-view unknown constraints are not closed. Do not default unknown to immobile.
- **Canonical/persistence:** candidate face ownership, candidate rig and MIRA decoded weights do not automatically make qualified canonical product IR. The product needs intervention tests, artifact binding, and replay.

## E. Minimal decisive proof suite

- **Topo ownership:** under identical source 3D, compare candidate mesh incidence/connected components + role logits against source component boundaries and editable chart cut consequences. Include body-skin seams and disconnected multi-mesh held objects as negatives.
- **Socket:** author/native source parent/socket and matching motion for one true grip; add a same-distance but unheld decoy. Require pointed AXIS socket/anchor + candidate held/holder + geometric frame + held-out rigid transport. Reverse holder/held → fail; remove source intervention → abstain; rotate object 45° and drag tip → exact anchor and solve residuals.
- **Graphics:** render front/back/source 8-view oracle under 3 pose changes; measure first-hit owner, alpha, tint, tie policy, revealed-surface uncertainty, missed seam, and original-vs-generated pixel authority. No interpolation of hidden source under a false original tag.
- **Secondary:** one native temporal appendage with pin and pose source, one rigid prop and one still body-negative; preserve source ground truth and distinguish policy-generated motion; verify deterministic time stepping, reset and controls.
- **Integrated:** one checkpoint (or training-free hand-authored *native* evidence case for earlier consumer proof), one parent mesh/face/vertex identity, one model inference proposal, Compiler court and editor/runtime replay. Separate successful mocks from true source/native and true learned checkpoints.
