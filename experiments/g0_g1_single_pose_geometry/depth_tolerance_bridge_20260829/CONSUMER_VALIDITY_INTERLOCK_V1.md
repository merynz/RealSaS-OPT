## Why this exists

The current structured-depth bridge can validly measure robustness of observable geometry,
teacher-free persistence, the typed Compiler surface boundary, and any already-frozen current proxy bytes.
It cannot by itself establish the final product-safe IRIS tolerance because the intended learned
Geppetto and Arachne consumers are not yet qualified on the RealSaS 2.5D rigging substrate.

Open literature is an architecture prior, not evidence that a 3D-trained skeleton/skinning model
will transfer unchanged to an 8-view observable shell plus closed rigging-volume substrate.

## Rule

The current bridge may continue and may publish a versioned **surface/substrate tolerance chart**.
Before that chart is used as the final product acceptance region for foundation-prior/IRIS selection,
close a cheap consumer-validity gate:

1. deterministic `ClosedRiggingVolume V0` / `InteriorRiggingSubstrate V0`;
2. minimal `Geppetto G0` consuming the actual substrate;
3. Compiler skeleton qualification;
4. minimal `Arachne A0` consuming `G0` + surface/volume evidence;
5. Compiler skin qualification + deformation/proof replay on clean substrate;
6. explicit coupling probes so A0 cannot silently compensate a systematically bad G0 skeleton.

G0/A0 need not be product-final. They must be real consumers whose response to substrate corruption
is plausible and whose clean-substrate behavior passes a preregistered minimum gate.

## Version consequence

A product-level tolerance object is consumer-profile specific.

`R_product[G0,A0] != R_product[G1,A1]` in general.

If Geppetto or Arachne is retrained/replaced, product-level depth qualification must be replayed under
the new consumer profile before previous model rankings transfer.

## Metric authority

Component metrics remain diagnostic:
- IRIS/persistence: ray-aligned and projected geometry residuals;
- Geppetto: projected joint/bone-corridor and legality diagnostics;
- Arachne: deformation/silhouette/contact response rather than weight-L2 alone.

The final authority remains end-to-end frozen replay/proof, not any one intermediate metric.

## Firewall

This interlock does **not** authorize parallel full research programs for IRIS, Geppetto, and Arachne.
It authorizes one cheap end-to-end consumer skeleton before final IRIS model selection.
Current M2/M3 bridge apparatus remains the execution frontier.
