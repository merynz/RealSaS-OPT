# VERY IMPORTANT AUDIT — M4 IDENTITY, AMBIGUITY & EQUIVALENT SUBSTRATE

**Date:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** CANONICAL RESEARCH AUDIT / DO-NOT-DELETE  
**Scope:** Historical M4 identity lineage → Graph V1 / QuotientFormer V1/V2 → current IRIS observable-geometry reframing.  
**Critical wording correction:** **observable evidence was not pruned; the problem was pruned.** The current observation contract is in several respects richer and more explicit than the old exact-owner formulation.

## 0. Executive decision

The long M4 lineage does **not** support the claim that static multi-view raster observations contain no useful identity/mechanical signal. It supports a narrower conclusion:

1. M4 learned real and partially transferable identity-related signal.
2. Exact hidden authored-owner recovery was repeatedly brittle under family-disjoint inference.
3. A concrete latent-slot architecture, Graph V1, was shown to be **identity-contracting** on a real family.
4. QuotientFormer V1 did **not** test the intended joint two-pose quotient hypothesis; its final consumer reduced the problem to per-pose scalar SAME affinity + one threshold + connected components, with separate admission.
5. Typed signed joint quotient solving survived learning-free closure; CORR was causally necessary; V2-C shared typed tiny closure passed with strong safety/equivariance checks.
6. Those successes were **not family-disjoint proof**. Full V2 training was correctly left unauthorized pending V2-D.
7. The current IRIS direction therefore does not declare M4 solved. It changes the target from privileged hidden mechanical identity to **rich observable persistent surface evidence**, while preserving ambiguity rather than forcing unsupported singleton identity.

The remaining decisive question is downstream sufficiency:

> Can the richer observable contract — P, geometric N, observation support/visibility, uncertainty/risk, coarse+fine persistence, set-valued ambiguity and provenance — support construction of a RigAnything-like **2.5D equivalent rigging substrate** well enough for Geppetto, even when some hidden information is genuinely ambiguous or unextractable?

That question must be tested on the geometric corpus before this reframing is treated as product-level closure.

## 1. What the old M4 program actually established

The August 10 lineage audit reconstructed at least 65 experiment records, 16 incidents and 27 checkpoint lines across V18.81–V19.30, R-series, V5–V7 and related causal diagnostics. Its narrowest diagnosis was not "candidate missing" or "compiler incapable": current S1 generally retained legal owner alternatives; truth mechanical coordinates could separate them; exact relation signs plus deterministic solving could close exact partitions. The break occurred when learned image-derived evidence had to become owner-consistent mechanical identity on unseen families.

This matters because it separates four statements that were too often conflated:

- **signal exists in static raster** — supported in several experiments;
- **candidate support contains the answer** — often supported;
- **a deterministic solver can consume exact/model-shaped identity evidence** — strongly supported;
- **the learned image representation can recover one privileged hidden author-owner partition family-disjoint** — not closed.

The correct historical conclusion is therefore **representation/factorization/consumer failure under an over-demanding identity target**, not an observability impossibility theorem.

## 2. Graph V1: a real architecture was falsified

### 2.1 Real-family binding

The local collapse proof on family `16634` used:

- 96 proposals;
- 32 exact owners;
- 68 active candidates;
- canonical projection parity RMS `2.978e-08`, max `1.192e-07`.

So the diagnostic was not explained by a gross projection mismatch.

### 2.2 Exact quotient was sufficient downstream

Exact one-hot owner assignment Γ plus exact parent authority achieved:

- relation accuracy `1.000` on active valid pairs;
- relation accuracy `1.000` on 110 hard near-coincident `d<0.10` SAME/PARENT/CHILD pairs.

Thus the downstream algebra itself could represent the desired solution.

### 2.3 Learned refinement contracted identity

Under the trained refinement:

- centered H RMS: `0.00253003 → 0.00054564`;
- mean off-diagonal H cosine: `0.999871 → 0.999999`;
- correct-owner Γ mass: `0.01397 → 0.00788`;
- hard `d<0.10` relation: `0.000` correct, `1.000` predicted DISTINCT.

At epsilon exactly zero, identity remained at numerical-noise scale. Candidate-grounded perturbation at the same budget did not rescue it. Even a deliberately easier assignment-only tiny overfit failed to produce usable owner identity; freezing the initial Hungarian map did not rescue it either.

### 2.4 Bounded verdict

**Falsified:** this Graph V1 latent-slot / referent-birth / refinement mechanism.

**Not falsified:** static observability, quotient representation in general, or the whole RealSaS architecture.

The lesson carried forward is structural: do not expect an anonymous symmetric latent slot state to manufacture persistent referent identity through a contracting refinement path.

## 3. QuotientFormer V1: the intended hypothesis was not actually tested

The intended scientific idea was a joint quotient over A∪B with cross-pose correspondence support. The final V1 consumer instead became:

`single-pose scalar SAME affinity → one global threshold → connected components → Q`

with a separately learned admission threshold. A and B were trained/evaluated separately; CORR was diagnostic rather than final authority.

Therefore V1 did **not** falsify the intended joint block quotient:

`[[S_AA, S_AB], [S_BA, S_BB]]`.

It did falsify / weaken:

- the scalar pair consumer as sufficient exact authority;
- one global scalar partition threshold;
- the separate learned admission threshold;
- the idea that very high AUC is enough for exact sparse partition recovery.

The full run repeatedly had `exact_quotient_partition_rate = 0.0`. The training finished with best step 1000; the best selection key contained hard-same recall about `0.483` and cross-pose same recall about `0.778`, yet exact quotient remained zero. Later checkpoints often became extremely conservative: hard-distinct accuracy could reach 1.0 while hard-same recall collapsed near zero. This is a classic example of a good-looking pair metric failing the actual combinatorial object.

A historical all-in-one collapse also occurred at some checkpoints, further demonstrating that partition topology — not mean pair AUC — is the correct object of evaluation.

## 4. V2 closure: what survived

### 4.1 V2-A — signed joint quotient solver

A typed signed joint solver with explicit admission, equivalence and CANNOT constraints passed the learning-free closure ladder. On the four fit-family regression set it remained `4/4 exact` under typed oracle evidence.

**Conclusion:** the quotient representation and deterministic typed consumer survived. The old scalar consumer did not.

### 4.2 V2-B — CORR is causal, not decorative

Removing CORR changed Q on every tested family and produced large errors/oversplitting. The causal ablation remained `4/4 nonzero` on the regression families.

**Conclusion:** bounded cross-view/cross-pose persistence evidence is a real causal channel.

### 4.3 V2-C — shared typed tiny closure

Mandatory real tiny families closed exactly after the shared typed evidence + solver refinement:

- `16634`: CORR ON trace `28`, pair error `0`, connectivity `21/21`; CORR OFF trace `47`, pair error `199`, connectivity `2/21`.
- `16638`: CORR ON trace `20`, pair error `0`, connectivity `17/17`; CORR OFF trace `31`, pair error `220`, connectivity `6/17`.

Safety audit:

- 16634 certified intra MUST edges `183`, false certified MUST `0`;
- 16638 certified intra MUST edges `267`, false certified MUST `0`;
- false reciprocal CORR bridges vetoed `225/225` and `176/176`;
- true reciprocal SAME falsely hard-CANNOT `0`;
- node false-active / missed-active `0/0` on both mandatory families.

Equivariance:

- candidate permutation joint-Q max error `0.0`;
- A↔B pose-swap joint-Q max error `0.0`.

### 4.4 UNKNOWN safety refinement

A naive joint-admission replay exposed one precise hole: one weak CORR MAY edge could activate an otherwise unsupported ambiguous candidate. The generic repair was:

`UNKNOWN_ACTIVATION_SUPPORT_MIN = 2`

One support unit cannot activate an UNKNOWN hard-component; two independent support units may.

After that global rule, admission error returned to zero on both mandatory tiny families.

**Important:** this proves UNKNOWN/ambiguity was already present in the old research language. The new direction did **not invent UNKNOWN**.

## 5. What is actually new about the current ambiguity policy

The novelty is not the word `UNKNOWN`, nor the existence of top-k candidates. Both existed historically in some form.

The deeper change is where ambiguity sits in the contract.

### Old pressure

The old M4 formulation ultimately wanted the perception stack to collapse observations into one privileged hidden mechanical-owner identity/partition early enough that downstream stages could treat it as authority.

Even when UNKNOWN existed, the evaluation pressure remained dominated by exact hidden-owner recovery.

### Current policy

The current IRIS program allows the observation layer to terminate in a **qualified, set-valued observable statement**:

- common-frame surface locus P;
- geometric normal N;
- observation support/visibility;
- predictive uncertainty/risk;
- coarse high-recall persistence evidence;
- fine local persistence evidence;
- reciprocal/cycle consistency;
- a candidate set when top-1 is unsupported;
- exact provenance back to view/pixel/evidence.

It does **not** have to name the hidden authored bone/owner.

Therefore the problem has been pruned from:

`static image → privileged exact hidden mechanical identity`

to:

`static multi-view image → maximally justified persistent observable surface substrate`.

The evidence was not pruned. It is richer and more explicitly uncertainty-aware.

## 6. Why RigAnything is relevant — and why it is not a proof

The code/paper audit of RigAnything highlighted two crucial facts:

1. successful auto-rigging is not a pure neural forward pass; deterministic post-processing remains important;
2. ambiguity is treated generatively — multiple skeletons/topologies may be valid for the same shape rather than one privileged hidden rig being forced as unique truth.

This undermines the old inference:

> "RigAnything can rig a 3D mesh, therefore our raster observation should uniquely recover the teacher's exact hidden owner partition."

What RigAnything actually demonstrates is weaker but valuable:

> rich geometry can support generation of a **usable equivalent mechanical structure** without proving uniqueness of one authored hidden rig.

That is the correct comparison target for the new RealSaS program.

# 7. VERY IMPORTANT OPEN TEST — EQUIVALENT 2.5D SUBSTRATE

**This section is mandatory. The current observable-geometry reframing must not be promoted to product truth without this test program.**

The geometric corpus now gives RealSaS the apparatus to test the claim directly.

## Gate E0 — exact observable substrate ceiling

Question:

> If exact legal observable P/N/support/persistence is supplied, can a deterministic SurfaceBuilder form a clean canonical 2.5D substrate that retains the rig-relevant structure?

Do not use hidden owner IDs as input. They may be used only for downstream evaluation.

Required outputs should be observation-grounded objects such as:

- persistent physical surface loci;
- local neighborhoods/graph;
- sheets/components;
- visibility/support summaries;
- normals/local differential geometry;
- uncertainty/ambiguity sets;
- view provenance.

This is the closest 2.5D analogue of the geometry substrate a 3D auto-rigger receives.

## Gate E1 — Geppetto oracle-substrate sufficiency

Feed **exact observable substrate only** to Geppetto.

Measure whether Geppetto can recover a clean, functionally equivalent skeleton/hierarchy to the level required by RealSaS — not the teacher's arbitrary exact bone IDs.

Required comparison:

1. exact observable substrate;
2. exact observable substrate minus N;
3. minus persistence;
4. minus support/provenance;
5. ambiguity collapsed incorrectly to top-1;
6. ambiguity retained set-valued.

If exact observable substrate is insufficient, the current IRIS target contract is missing information and must be revised **before blaming the learner**.

## Gate E2 — extractability

Question:

> Is the information used by the successful E1 substrate actually extractable from the legal A×8 raster observation?

Run the IRIS controlled line:

`A×8 → learned P/N/U/Z → deterministic top-k/reciprocal/cycle → set-valued tracks → qualified substrate`.

Evaluate channel fidelity and, more importantly, downstream substrate consequence.

A low pixel/feature error is not enough if the error destroys a sheet/component or creates a false cross-surface bridge.

## Gate E3 — predicted-substrate Geppetto sufficiency

Replace exact observable evidence with predicted IRIS evidence while keeping Geppetto fixed.

Measure the **downstream performance gap**:

`Geppetto(exact observable substrate)` vs `Geppetto(predicted observable substrate)`.

This localizes whether failure belongs to:

- substrate definition;
- IRIS learner;
- deterministic evidence wrapper;
- Geppetto.

## Gate E4 — ambiguity stress / information genuinely absent

Construct or identify cases where legal observations do not support a singleton correspondence or unique surface continuation.

Test three policies:

1. forced top-1;
2. UNKNOWN/abstain only;
3. calibrated set-valued top-k with uncertainty and consistency constraints.

Primary question:

> When some information is not uniquely extractable, does preserving the legal hypothesis set still allow Geppetto + later verification to produce a clean functionally equivalent rig?

If yes, non-identifiability of hidden details is **not** a product blocker.

If no, determine exactly which missing observable variable is required.

## Gate E5 — RigAnything-like 2.5D equivalence test

Do not compare exact topology IDs. Compare whether the produced 2.5D substrate supports the same class of downstream mechanical work that a 3D surface substrate supports:

- stable joint placement;
- hierarchy proposal;
- skinning/weighting support;
- deformation quality;
- editability;
- compiler verification/repair;
- robustness across family and morphology.

The target is:

`functionally equivalent rigging substrate`, not `exact reconstruction of the source author's hidden rig`.

## 8. Failure taxonomy for the new tests

Every failure in E0–E5 must be classified before architecture changes:

1. **apparatus/data failure** — wrong renderer, correspondence truth, transform, split, loader or evaluator;
2. **representation failure** — the legal observation contains needed information but P/N/support/persistence language loses it;
3. **target-authority failure** — the requested target is not the right observable/product invariant;
4. **learner failure** — exact representation succeeds, predicted evidence fails;
5. **consumer failure** — observable substrate is adequate but SurfaceBuilder/Geppetto consumes it incorrectly;
6. **genuine information limit** — only after the above are excluded.

A learner failure is not an information-limit proof. A hidden-teacher ambiguity is not a product failure if multiple observation-compatible rigs are functionally equivalent.

## 10. Current commander's intent

The controlled IRIS line should first answer the easier, lawful question:

> Can we recover persistent observable surface geometry when camera, raster authority and physical correspondence are exact?

If that cannot be solved under these favorable conditions, the failure must be localized in apparatus, representation, learner or evidence-consumer design.

If it succeeds, robustness expands in order:

1. controlled exact-camera geometry;
2. camera jitter with known true camera;
3. camera jitter with camera estimated/uncertain;
4. appearance/domain variation;
5. artist-specific cross-view inconsistency;
6. product-domain equivalent-substrate qualification.

Do not reopen privileged exact-owner M4 reconstruction merely because uncertainty appears. Preserve uncertainty and test whether the downstream product actually needs the missing hidden fact.
