## Purpose

Freeze a concrete carrier-level collision metric before inspecting revised-H collision results.

The audit uses the revised same-pool `final_k=16` candidate representation demonstrated by Test C. Truth is target/evaluator only and is not included in representation distance.

## Inference-available carrier representation

For every raster-derived Problem-A carrier, construct:

1. `Z_A`: unit-normalized frozen descriptor consensus (64-D); descriptor distance is cosine distance.
2. `P_A`: reconstructed common-world position (3-D).
3. `N_A`: frozen N1D normal consensus (3-D).
4. `V_A`: eight binary view-support indicators (8-D).
5. current differential evidence from frozen `delta_point_map_srcA`:
   - consensus delta vector (3-D),
   - `log(eps + ||delta||)` (1-D).
6. revised feasible-set `H_i` summaries computed from all pairwise rank-3 hypotheses generated by coarse-top8 / same refined pool / final-top16:
   - `log(1+|H_i|)`;
   - relative-H centroid `mean(H_i-P_A)` (3-D);
   - covariance eigenvalues of `H_i-P_A`, sorted descending (3-D);
   - displacement-magnitude quantiles q10/q25/q50/q75/q90 (5-D);
   - mean unit displacement direction / resultant vector (3-D).
7. per-view descriptor-candidate uncertainty summaries, aggregated over visible views:
   - median top1-top2 cosine-score margin;
   - median top1-top16 score spread;
   - median normalized softmax entropy over retained candidates;
   - visible-view count.

No teacher/sidecar field enters these features.

## Distance

Split the representation into descriptor and non-descriptor blocks.

- Descriptor distance: `d_Z = 1 - cosine(Z_i, Z_j)`.
- All non-descriptor continuous/binary fields are robust-scaled over the truth-open development panel using median/IQR; constant fields are ignored. Define `d_O` as RMS Euclidean distance over the retained scaled dimensions.
- Composite distance is fixed as:

```text
d_repr = sqrt(d_Z^2 + d_O^2)
```

Nearest-neighbor retrieval is **cross-family only**.

## Target differences

Evaluator maps each reconstructed `P_A` to canonical observable target using the same Hungarian/persistent mapping semantics as GFDR evaluation.

For target motion `delta* = P_B* - P_A*`:

- `active* = ||delta*|| > 0.005`;
- amplitude difference is the ratio of larger to smaller active amplitude;
- direction similarity is cosine between unit target deltas when both are active.

## Collision flags

Evaluate the closest **5%** and **10%** of cross-family nearest-neighbor distances. A pair is a material target collision when any of the following holds:

1. active/silent state differs;
2. both active and amplitude ratio is **> 3.0x**;
3. both active and direction cosine is **< 0.50** (more than 60 degrees apart).

Report each flag separately and their union. Also report median direction cosine and median log-amplitude difference without thresholding.

## Decision semantics

- **RED:** repeated material collisions occur in the closest 5% tail and inspection shows the colliding pairs are not separated by any already-proposed explicit factor/uncertainty field represented above; or the union collision fraction is >=20% in the closest 5% with at least two distinct cross-family collision families.
- **GREEN:** closest 5% union collision fraction <10%, with no repeated unresolved collision family, and closest-neighbor target direction/amplitude remain coherent.
- **AMBER:** between those conditions, or collisions are specifically attributable to a factor already proposed but not yet instantiated (for example a learned/calibrated `p_active`).

These thresholds are diagnostics, not product acceptance gates.
