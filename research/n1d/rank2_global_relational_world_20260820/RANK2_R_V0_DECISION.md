## Diagnosis

The failure is not optimizer non-uniqueness and is not evidence against global relational addressing in general. The V0 lift used raw cross-pose 3D relation equality as the dominant relational semantics: the selected Pose-B world was rewarded for resembling the Pose-A pair geometry. Under real articulation, the correct B world is *supposed* to change many pair relations. V0 therefore imposed the wrong invariance and often pulled a geometrically closer world into a mechanically worse realization.

The observable-only precursor already foreshadowed the scale/semantics problem: selected V0 relational energy was worse than the frozen baseline relational energy in 8/8 families even while the combined objective was lower under its unary trade-off.

## Scientific consequence

V0 falsifies **raw A-vs-B pair-relation equality + uncalibrated G/R sum** as the Rank-2 lift. It does not falsify the user's R concept. Historical R's semantic invariant remains useful: identity/world state must be judged by a carrier's address in the complete relational structure rather than a local neighbour signature.
